How it works

Every card is 24 squares picked at random from 60 generic dating-app clichés, plus a free square in the middle. Mark a full row, column or diagonal for bingo. Mark all 25 for a blackout.

Nothing you tap leaves your browser. There are no accounts, cookies or trackers. Your marks live in this page and in the link, but only if you copy it and send it yourself. The link holds the card number and which squares are marked, nothing else.

The squares are made-up tropes, not real people or real profiles. This is a joke game for adults who use dating apps, so it is 18+.
